TotalSegmentator Wrapper for Mac bundled Sample 1 provenance and notices

Sample purpose
- This bundled sample is for non-clinical UI preview and local workflow testing.
- It is not for diagnosis, treatment planning, quantitative accuracy evaluation,
  or any other clinical use.
- The bundled sample is not DICOM. It contains a derived NIfTI input,
  a precomputed tooth labelmap, and offline browser-preview artifacts.

Source data and publication authorization
- The source is a CT of the project rights holder.
- The rights holder explicitly authorized public distribution of the derived
  jaw-region NIfTI, model output, and preview artifacts.
- The authorization decision is recorded in
  docs/43_OPEN_SOURCE_PUBLICATION_DECISIONS.md in the source repository.
- The raw DICOM is not committed, bundled in the app or DMG, uploaded to a
  release, or deployed on the public website.

TotalSegmentator Wrapper for Mac handling
- TotalSegmentator Wrapper for Mac is an unofficial Mac wrapper powered by
  TotalSegmentator. It is not the official TotalSegmentator application or project.
- TotalSegmentator Wrapper for Mac does not send DICOM, CT, processing results,
  or generated 3D files during setup or 3D preview creation.
- The bundled sample is included only to make the first-run experience usable
  before the user chooses their own local data.
